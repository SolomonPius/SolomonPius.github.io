Now · Undergraduate thesis · CLIMB Lab

Hop-and-roll locomotion for a hopping quadcopter

With Prof. Pakpong Chirarattananon, I'm designing a multi-legged tensegrity structure that lets a hopping quadcopter roll from leg to leg between hops, so it keeps moving after rough landings instead of crashing.

Read about the thesis ↓
  • Simulation baseline: RK4 tensegrity simulator in MATLAB. 0.73 coefficient of restitution at a 3.5 m/s landing.
  • Next: extending to multi-hop, leg-to-leg rolling.
  • Learning: a dynamics model trained on simulated and experimental data to plan hop trajectories and landing control, borrowing sim-to-real methods from legged robots.
